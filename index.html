1. Basics

All equipment has a specific rarity (color) associated with it.

  • Equipment can come from Shop crates as Common (white), Uncommon (green), Rare (blue), or Epic (purple).
  • Equipment can be upgraded to Legendary (orange), Mythic (red), and ??? (???).
  • The highest rarities of equipment contain multiple sub-levels called "plus levels" (denoted by the '+' icon).

2. Merging

Merging allows the player to combine multiple equipment of a certain rarity, turning it into a single equipment of a higher rarity (or level).

4. Equipment Shop

  • Rare Crate: 1 free pull every 24h; 50 gems per pull
  • Epic Crate: 1 free pull every 7d; 100 gems per pull
  • Chromatic Crate: 1 free pull per account; 120 gems per pull (1200 gems for x10 pull)
  • Rare crates can be opened with rare crate keys, which are in game currency dropped during battle.
  • There is no bulk discount for pulling x10 on the Chromatic crate (unlike Chips, where pulling x10 saves 50 gems).
  • The Chromatic crate has pity mechanics for both Epic and S-Epic, which are displayed in game on the crate banner.
